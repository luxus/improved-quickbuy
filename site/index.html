Improved Quickbuy

Shows the souls you're still missing for your queued items, right in the Deadlock HUD. Works with CompLock HUD++ and without it.

Add to DMMInstall with DMMDownload VPK

The buttons need Deadlock Mod Manager. Source URL for DMM (Settings → Mods → Mod sources → Add source):
https://luxus.github.io/improved-quickbuy/dmm-source.json

Manual install: rename the VPK to a free slot such as pak03_dir.vpk and put it into Deadlock/game/citadel/addons/.
